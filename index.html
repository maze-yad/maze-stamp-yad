Stamp 08 of 11 · Slemani

The city on a stamp

The horseman statue is one of the first things people picture when they think of Slemani. Here he stands under fresh snow, ringed by pines, printed in the same warm red as the festival's postmark.

Why we chose it

A festival belongs to a place. Putting the city's best-known landmark at the heart of the identity tells everyone, at a glance, that these are Slemani's Christmas Nights.

Stamp 06 of 11 · Halparke

Hands joined, feet in the snow

Halparke is the dance that turns a crowd into a line of friends. Three dancers in full traditional dress lead it here, coins catching the light, a scarf swinging overhead.

Why we chose it

Christmas Nights is about celebrating together. The deep greens and reds of the costumes are the brand palette itself, so tradition and festival speak with one voice.

Stamp 10 of 11 · Kew

A familiar winter visitor

The kew, the chukar partridge of the Kurdish hills, turns up again and again in local songs and stories. On its stamp it stands proud in the snow, its striped wings drawn in fine engraved lines.

Why we chose it

We wanted nature that feels local rather than imported. The kew gives the set a gentle, homegrown character, sitting right beside Santa and the reindeer.

Stamp 03 of 11 · Wild Goat

Up on the peaks

A wild goat holds its ground on a snowy summit, horns sweeping back like the mountain ridges behind it. It is the boldest stamp in the set and the one with the most attitude.

Why we chose it

The mountains around the city shape its winters. The goat brings that strength and sense of height into a brand that could otherwise feel only cosy.

Stamp 09 of 11 · Old Houses

Every window lit

Stone houses stacked up a hillside, snow on every roof, smoke curling from the chimneys and stained glass glowing gold and red from inside.

Why we chose it

The festival is called Christmas Nights, and nothing says night like a lit window. These houses carry the warmth of the evening and the idea of coming home.

Stamp 04 of 11 · Christmas Tree

The one everyone knows

Red baubles, gold lights, a star on top and presents waiting underneath. The classic tree, drawn with the same engraved texture as the rest of the set.

Why we chose it

Some symbols need no explaining. The tree makes the festival readable to anyone, from any distance, and lets the local stamps around it feel like part of the season.
